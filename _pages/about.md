I am an Economic Researcher at [Banco de México](https://www.banxico.org.mx/), in the Directorate General of Economic Research. I hold a Ph.D. in Demography from the University of California, Berkeley (2024), where my dissertation examined Mexican migration in the 21st century, and a Graduate Certificate in Applied Data Science.

My research sits at the intersection of migration and economic demography, using large-scale administrative and survey data to study how people move, who moves, and how migration shapes households and labor markets in Mexico. Previously, I was a Guest Researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research and a graduate student researcher on the NIH-funded CenSoc Project at UC Berkeley.
